# Research Log Guide

Use this guide when the user wants important papers recorded into a research log.

## When to write to a research log

Write a log entry when a paper is:
- especially relevant,
- likely to influence future experiments,
- worth comparing against repeatedly,
- a strong baseline or conceptual anchor.

## Minimal entry format

```markdown
### [YYYY-MM-DD] Paper Title
- Authors: ...
- Link: ...
- PDF: ...
- Why it matters: ...
- One key insight: ...
```

## Rules

- Keep entries concise and searchable.
- Focus on why the paper matters.
- Avoid dumping the full deep-read report into the log.
